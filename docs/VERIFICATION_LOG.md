## Corrections after full extraction (Task P, 2026-10-02; these supersede the table above where they differ)

1. The high-volatility FX currency list in v2.8+2506 has no ISK: ARS, EGP, ETB, GHS, LBP, NGN, RUB, SCR, VES, ZMW. v2.8+2512 added ISK and dropped RUB and VES.
2. FX risk weight is one 2x2 table in both versions (row: the given currency's FX volatility group; column: the calculation currency's group). v2.8+2506: regular/regular 7.1, regular/high 18.0, high/regular 18.0, high/high 30.6. v2.8+2512: 7.4, then 31.7 for all other combinations. With USD as calculation currency a high-vol currency therefore gets 18.0 (2506) or 31.7 (2512).
3. 12 CFR 45.8: the backtesting requirement is paragraph (f)(2)(ii)-(iii), not (f)(3). 12 CFR 45.8 has no quarterly backtesting requirement; "at least every 3 months" is EU 2016/2251 Art 14(3) only (US row is NOT_SPECIFIED).
4. The 25% stress share and the 3 to 5 year window are EU Art 16 rules. The US rule is 1 to 5 years with a stress period and no percentage. BCBS-IOSCO gives only a 5 year maximum.
5. BCBS-IOSCO Appendix A has no cross-currency swap rows; those (1/2/4%) are in 12 CFR 45 Appendix A.
6. The FX correlation 2x2 tables have non-unit diagonals and are not positive semidefinite on their own; the expanded factor-level matrices are.
7. Structure for the engine: IR vega has no separate expiry correlation table or formula (K uses the same 12x12 tenor matrix with f = 1; inflation-vol vs IR-vol correlation 42%; gamma 35%); IR vega sigma is the market ATM vol; HVR_IR enters only the curvature scale (HVR_IR^-2). FX vega sigma = RW*sqrt(365/14)/Phi^-1(0.99). The IR "regular" risk-weight group has 14 currencies; "well-traded" (USD, EUR, GBP) is a concentration-threshold group only.
8. Min eigenvalue of the IR tenor matrix is 0.005015 in both versions; psi matrices are PSD (min eigenvalue 0.331 for 2512, 0.317 for 2506).
9. Still unverified: ISDA credit, equity and commodity tables; the IR vega expiry correlation reading rests on the methodology text; EU consolidated amendments; 12 CFR 237.8 and 349.8; CFTC 23.154.
